## Chosen product in one page

**User and trigger.** A district/block organiser or NSS programme officer has a concrete drive, an opt-in candidate list and a closing host report. Public MY Bharat material shows discovery, attendance, credentials, impact tracking and an internal dashboard; its exact private rostering capabilities are **NOT FOUND** in the pages reviewed. Position this as a possible plug-in workflow to validate with a programme owner, not a replacement. [MY Bharat about](https://mybharat.gov.in/about_mybharat), [PIB monitoring reply](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2222299&lang=1&reg=6).

**Core loop.** (1) An LLM reads an English/Hindi/Hinglish task brief and drafts required roles with source spans and unresolved questions. The organiser edits/approves the roles. (2) A constraint solver considers opt-in skills, availability, broad area and safety gates, then returns an explainable roster, qualified backup and *unfilled slots*. (3) “Drop one person” runs a genuine counterfactual; the UI displays the new crew or a red “mission not ready” state and the exact missing capability. (4) After the event, an LLM drafts a contribution summary from synthetic host notes; a deterministic check compares claimed and attendance hours. A host corrects or rejects; export includes the source and review status. No card is called independently verified. AI is central to translating messy field language into a decision-ready specification; deterministic search decides assignments.

**Numerical story.** The existing rerunnable synthetic harness gives must-role coverage **0.917** for CP-SAT, nearest-skill and backup-aware manual-greedy; dropout robustness **0.760, 0.153, 0.760** respectively; repeat-pick share **0.230 vs 0.427** manual. These are *prototype fixture* results, not a UP effect. The v2 proof is an **independently written challenge set and timed organiser baseline**, both still **NOT MEASURED**. Show the six misses, especially two under-supplied tasks. See `../EVAL_RESULTS.md` and `STRATEGY-REVIEW.md`.

**Why a jury may remember it — INFERENCE:** instead of another opportunity feed, a judge sees the moment an attractive roster fails a stress test and an official sees the reason for saying “delay this mission or recruit one trained person.” It connects AI extraction, hard constraints, human authority and an auditable closeout to one decision. The selection panel explicitly rewards inventiveness in difficult aspects and measurable outcome [on its official page](https://www.inmobi.com/ai-kaushal). No top-five probability can be justified without rival entries or field evidence.

**24-hour boundary.** One organiser, one task, 20 synthetic **adult** opt-in profiles, role review, one solver preflight, one dropout, one host-reviewed conflicting card, a small CSV/JSON export and a labelled comparison screen. Use provided model credits for extraction and note drafting if available; pin replay for the finals and show one cold-call test offstage. Keep existing tested solver/rules if organisers explicitly permit prior code. Otherwise rebuild only this slice from the specification. Cut marketplace, WhatsApp bot, voice, district heatmap, credential issuer, uploaded photos, real minors, live MY Bharat access and broad chatbot. `FEATURES-v2.md` and `PLAN-v2.md` make the gates explicit.
