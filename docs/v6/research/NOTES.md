- Igloo: monochrome, one 3D object that morphs per scroll section (igloo -> crystal -> ring); almost no text; fog/depth.
- Bruno Simon: a playable 3D toy scene; the interaction IS the site.
- Lusion: clean light page, big reel, work grid; restraint around one bold 3D moment.
- Apple AirPods/Vision Pro: one statement per screen ("The best thing you've never heard"), product on white, huge type 80-96 px, video.
- Darkroom / Aristide / Rauno: type as the visual (250-720 px), strict grids, mono accents.
- Stripe / Linear: product UI as hero, numbers band ("135+ / US$1.9tn / 99.999%"), calm gradients, dense but ordered.
- charity: water / Ocean Cleanup: impact storytelling = real human photo + ONE big fact ("696 million people lack basic access...") + one action button.
- The Pudding / OWID: data storytelling; each story is a bold colour card; charts are the hero.
- Monopo: full-bleed gradient field + big type; Poolsuite: a whole OS metaphor (strong single concept).

## Early lessons vs our current site
1. One idea per screen. Ours stacks a paragraph card + backdrop + 8 tags + rail + labels on every screen.
2. Type is the main visual on top sites (80-250 px headlines, 3-8 words). Ours: ~60 px headline inside a boxed card, plus small paragraphs.
3. A single hero object/metaphor carried through the whole scroll (coaster, igloo, car). Ours swaps backdrops every chapter.
4. Real material looks premium; soft 720p AI film frames behind text read as cheap/blurry.
5. Impact sites lead with ONE big human number and ONE action.

## Awwwards batch A (55 scanned, 2 failed; several show "browser not supported"/bot walls in headless: Cyera, Santioni, Cartier, Lidar)
Strongest, and most relevant to us:
- Sweet Tape: CRISP paper-collage look (cut paper, tape, flat saturated colour) + giant condensed type with objects
  breaking through letters. Proof that "paper craft" can feel premium when it is crisp, flat and bold, not soft/blurry.
- Don't Look Up (game): hand-drawn crowd of tiny people around giant type; playful, human; the crowd is the story.
- Grail: giant serif headline (345 px), floating 3D cards, chaptered narrative ("It starts as one quest. It rarely stays that way.").
- Agrumea Farm: one huge circular shape with curved text + products orbiting it while you scroll -> a circle as the hero.
- Nick Ho / Zirka / Moto: giant numbers as visuals (98, 224 km/h, 0% FX) with tiny labels.
- Realevate / Colonia Zacamil: images set INSIDE headline words ("FLY TO [photo] ZACAMIL").
- PSP Production: full-bleed video + one sentence per screen ("We are movers." "We move forward.").
- Creative Reference Library / Grids / FORMAT: type and geometry only, strict grid, black/white + one colour.
Patterns: 1 statement per viewport; headline 90-345 px; max 2 typefaces (grotesk + serif or mono); one accent colour;
generous empty space; cookie banners everywhere (keep ours out); hero object or metaphor reused across the page.
