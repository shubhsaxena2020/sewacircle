How the Crew is Decided

Complete specification of solver constraints, plain-language metric definitions, scale benchmarks, and system boundaries.

TIER 1 // HARD CONSTRAINTS (FILTER PASS)

Every volunteer candidate must strictly satisfy 5 invariant rules before being admitted to the candidate pool:

1. Must-Have Skill: Matches the required skill taxonomy at or above the minimum required level.
2. Slot Availability: Confirmed available in the required calendar time slot (e.g. sat_morning).
3. Travel Proximity: Euclidean transit distance is strictly within the drive's max radius (e.g. 25 km).
4. Minor Eligibility: Volunteers under 18 admitted only where the brief explicitly permits minors with guardian consent.
5. Safety Role Verification: Safety-critical roles (first aid, disaster rescue, driving) ONLY accept host-checked skills from the database.
TIER 2 // CONSTRAINT SOLVER (OR-TOOLS CP-SAT)

Google OR-Tools CP-SAT formulation solves the primary roster while concurrently assigning a designated standby for every essential role:

  • Minimizes total travel distance across the primary crew.
  • Enforces novelty balancing: penalizes selecting volunteers who served in the preceding 90 days.
  • Enforces novice safety pairing: novices are paired with experienced host-checked mentors.
  • Guarantees single-dropout survivability: any single primary crew cancellation can be immediately healed by the standby pool without leaving an essential role vacant.
  • Benchmark timing on task-01: 15 ms, status: OPTIMAL.
METRIC DEFINITIONS IN PLAIN WORDS
Role Coverage

The percentage of essential volunteer roles required by the brief that have an assigned qualified volunteer.

Dropout Robustness

The percentage of essential roles that have an eligible standby volunteer ready to step in if the primary volunteer drops out.

Ready Rate

The percentage of volunteering drives that achieve both 100% role coverage and 100% single-dropout resilience.

Repeat-Pick Share

The proportion of volunteer slots filled by individuals who have already been selected for another drive in the last 90 days.

Exposure Gini

Measures opportunity inequality across the volunteer pool: 0 means every registered youth gets equal opportunity, while 1 means all volunteer slots go to a handful of repeat favorites.

HONEST BOUNDARIES & LIMITS

Sample Size Limits

Evaluated on 10 synthetic tasks and 80 volunteers in Uttar Pradesh. Does not constitute an operational field trial.

Statistical Boundary

Host-Checked Claims

Skills are host-checked operational claims recorded by drive organizers, not accredited government certificates.

Credential Scope

Ledger Proves Integrity, Not Truth

The cryptographic hash chain mathematically proves absence of tampering after recording; truth originates solely from human host confirmation.

Trust Model

AI Role Recall Limit

Role extraction recall is 0.932 (not 1.0). The organizer must inspect and confirm proposed roles on every drive.
