Honest Reading & Sample Limits

Evaluated on n=10 tasks with 80 volunteers. These numbers reflect synthetic constraint benchmarks, not an operational field trial. Confidence intervals are wide due to sample size.

Methodology Note

Why Misses Occur

In 7 of 10 tasks, random selection achieved marginally lower repeat-pick share because the CP-SAT solver prioritizes skill level and proximity before fairness tie-breaking.
