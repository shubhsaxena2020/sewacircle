FRAME 01 // DISTRICT YOUTH OPERATIONS

The step after Interested.

युवाओं ने रुचि दिखाई — अब ज़मीनी टीम तैयार करने और जोखिम मुक्त सेवा सुनिश्चित करने का वक्त।

SewaCircle Ready Crew is a desktop planning canvas for a district youth organiser in Uttar Pradesh. Young people have already shown interest in a volunteering drive on MY Bharat. The organiser pastes the drive brief (Hindi, English or Hinglish), AI proposes the roles needed (the organiser confirms), a constraint solver builds a crew with a standby for every essential role, and the tool answers one question: can this drive run? READY, or NOT READY plus exact recruit requests.

The National Platform Works

According to the PIB backgrounder (05 July 2026), over 2.22+ crore youth registered on MY Bharat and 1.52+ lakh volunteer opportunities have been posted nationwide.

MY Bharat aggregates interest across the country. SewaCircle solves what happens inside a district right after youth click Interested: skill verification, distance radius, slot availability, and standby redundancy.

Source: PIB Backgrounder, 05 July 2026

District Host Responsibility

Safety roles (first aid, logistics, local dialect liaison) only accept host-checked skills. An AI extraction suggests role counts; the human district organiser makes every final decision.

FRAME 02 // 6-STEP WHITEBOARD WORKFLOW

How a Drive Runs on the Whiteboard

From unformatted district brief text to verified post-drive credential ledger.

6 Automated & Human Checkpoints
STEP 01

Paste Brief

Organizer inputs Hindi, English, or Hinglish WhatsApp brief text.

STEP 02

AI Proposes Roles

LLM extracts roles with source text quotes, counts, and confidence.

STEP 03

Host Confirms

Host reviews safety roles (first aid), adjusts counts, and confirms.

STEP 04

CP-SAT Solver

Finds optimal crew + standby redundancy for every essential role.

STEP 05

Clear Verdict

READY: all covered. NOT READY: exact recruit requests for WhatsApp.

STEP 06

Post-Drive Ledger

Host notes, attendance check, and tamper-evident SHA-256 cards.

Scope Limitation

Host-Checked Claims

Skills are operational endorsements recorded by past hosts, not accredited government certificates.

Credential Bound

Ledger Proves Edits, Not Truth

Cryptographic hash chaining guarantees tamper-evident modification history, not objective real-world truth.

Integrity Definition

Bilingual Frontier Only

Extraction models tested on Hindi & English text. Local dialect nuances require organizer verification.
