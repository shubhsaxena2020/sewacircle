The Platform Gap

However, current public guidelines leave roster decisions to individual organiser discretion. If someone drops out on Saturday morning, or if no one has verified first-aid certification, the camp fails. SewaCircle fills this operational gap without displacing national systems.

Operational Workflow

How SewaCircle Ready Crew Works

AI reads unstructured text, a deterministic solver decides, a human organiser confirms, and a tamper-evident ledger preserves the truth.

1

Role Extraction with Privacy Redaction

Organisers paste briefs in Hindi, Hinglish, or English. All phone numbers, emails, and person names are scrubbed against a 61,000-name gazetteer before reaching the language model.

Offline Fallback: Qwen2.5-7B (4-bit) on one free T4 GPU reaches 0.932 role recall.
2

Deterministic Solver with Standbys

Google OR-Tools CP-SAT finds optimal crew assignments satisfying hard constraints: skills, travel distance, availability, minor consent, and strict host verification for safety roles.

Guaranteed Standby: Every critical role includes an eligible backup to survive single dropouts.
3

Evidence & Tamper-Evident Ledger

Post-drive, host notes are paired with attendance logs. An evidence rule flags discrepancies (e.g. round hour inflation), a host reviews, and a cryptographic hash chain logs every change.

Integrity Principle: The hash chain records edits; actual truth comes strictly from host sign-off.

Honest Boundaries

Product Limits & Safety Principles

Synthetic Seed 7 Data
Safety Roles Require Verification

First aid, disaster relief, and driving never accept self-declared skills. Only skills with documented host sign-off are considered by the solver.

Minor Protection (Ages 16–17)

Youth under 18 can only be scheduled for youth-permitted drives and only if signed guardian consent is verified on file.

No Auto-Verification

The tamper-evident ledger logs chronological edits and confirmations. It does not certify that work happened—the host signature is required.
