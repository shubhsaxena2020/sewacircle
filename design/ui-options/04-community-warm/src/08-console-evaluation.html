What is Dropout Robustness?

The percentage of must-slots where a backup volunteer with appropriate skills and travel radius is already confirmed. A score of 100% means any single dropout can be resolved instantly without canceling the camp.

What is Repeat Pick Share?

How often the planner re-picks volunteers who have volunteered in the last 90 days. CP-SAT reduces this by ~20 points compared to manual assignment, preventing volunteer fatigue and giving novices their first break.

What is Exposure Gini?

The classic inequality index (0 to 1). A Gini of 0 means every registered youth gets equal volunteering slots. CP-SAT keeps Gini at 0.4677, substantially lower than the 0.5081 seen in greedy manual selection.
