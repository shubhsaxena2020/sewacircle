What Happens Next

  3. Solver generates crew: OR-Tools CP-SAT computes the optimal roster with standby coverage and checks drop-out resilience.

Human-in-the-Loop Protocol

The AI proposes initial roles, but the district organizer makes all binding operational choices. Safety-critical positions (first-aid support and driving) strictly require host-checked capabilities.
