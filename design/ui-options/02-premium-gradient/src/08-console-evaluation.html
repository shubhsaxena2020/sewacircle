Plain-Language Metric Explanations

Exposure Gini Coefficient:

The exposure Gini coefficient measures inequality in volunteering assignments: a score closer to 0 indicates opportunities are shared evenly across registered youth, while higher scores show an unfair concentration on the same repeat participants.

Dropout Robustness:

The probability that any single volunteer dropping out at 7:00 AM on the morning of the drive can be instantaneously replaced by a verified standby without violating radius or skill rules.

Repeat-Pick Share:

Percentage of volunteer slots filled by people who have already served in recent drives. CP-SAT deliberately suppresses this to ensure newcomers participate.

Honest Reading: Sample Size & Statistical Significance

This evaluation reflects n=10 synthetic tasks and 80 volunteers. While the improvements in dropout robustness (+60.7%) and ready rate (+60.0%) are statistically significant on this benchmark dataset, confidence intervals remain wide (e.g. Robustness [50%, 96%]). This is an engineering benchmark, not an in-vivo field trial with real human participants.
