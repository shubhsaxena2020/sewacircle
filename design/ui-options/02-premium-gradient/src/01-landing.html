Six-Stage Architecture

How district crews get built and verified

A rigorous path from raw unstructured drive brief to verifiable post-event community credentialing.

1

Paste Drive Brief

Organizer inputs raw task brief in natural Hindi, English, or mixed Hinglish. Handles regional village nuances, expected attendance, and safety constraints.

Supports Devanagari
2

AI Proposes Roles

Extracts required volunteer capabilities with verbatim textual evidence spans, required headcounts, confidence scores, and identifies decision flags.

Recall 0.932 (T4)
3

Host Confirms Roster Spec

District organizer reviews AI extractions, selects target regional dialects (Awadhi/Bhojpuri), adjusts counts, and validates safety critical requirements.

Human in the loop
4

CP-SAT Builds Resilient Crew

Constraint satisfaction solver enforces hard limits: certified safety checks, radius limits, and pairs verified standbys to guarantee 1-dropout invariance.

15 ms solve time
5

Instant Verdict

Binary determination: READY or NOT READY. If not ready, generates exact shareable recruit requests for community WhatsApp groups.

Zero silent gaps
6

Audit & Ledger

Field notes cross-checked against registered attendance. Deterministic rule checks flag inflations; SHA-256 hash chain seals verified volunteer service cards.

Tamper-evident logs

Clear Boundaries

Honest Engineering Limits

What this system does and what it deliberately does not claim to do.

Synthetic Dataset Scope

All data is synthetically generated with seed 7. Tested across 10 tasks and 80 volunteers across 4 active districts in Uttar Pradesh. It is not an ongoing government pilot.

Checked Claims vs Certificates

Safety-critical roles (first aid, transport, medical support) require host-checked skills. A host check means a previous field organizer verified attendance, not an accredited diploma.

Hash Chain Proves Edits, Not Truth

The SHA-256 cryptographic ledger proves that contribution records have not been silently modified after the fact. Ground truth always originates from human host confirmation.

Language Scope & AI Extraction

Tested on Hindi, English, and Hinglish. Role extraction achieves 0.932 recall on an edge T4 model; the human organizer must always review and confirm extracted roles.
