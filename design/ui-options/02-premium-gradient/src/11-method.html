Algorithmic Formulation

How the Crew is Decided

Mathematical specification of constraint satisfaction, standby redundancy assignment, plain-language metric definitions, and honest system boundaries.

STAGE 1 • HARD CONSTRAINTS FILTER

Strict Feasibility Pruning

Before optimization begins, candidate pools are filtered through non-negotiable boundaries:

  • Skill Match: Must possess required skill at or above minimum level.
  • Availability: Volunteer must have registered the exact target time slot.
  • Travel Radius: Haversine distance from volunteer block to site ≤ 25.0 km.
  • Minor Protection: Youth < 18 permitted only if drive allows minors with guardian consent.
  • Safety Roles: First aid, transport, and site logistics strictly require host-checked claims.
STAGE 2 • MULTI-TIER OPTIMIZATION

CP-SAT Integer Programming

Google OR-Tools CP-SAT solves the assignment problem through hierarchical objective tiers:

  • Tier 1: Maximize coverage of all must-have role slots.
  • Tier 2: Maximize coverage of secondary should slots.
  • Tier 3: Fair rotation (penalize volunteers picked in last 90 days; prioritize newcomers).
  • Tier 4: Minimize total physical travel distance across the district.
STAGE 3 • INVARIANCE VERIFICATION

1-Dropout Redundancy Proof

Assigning primary volunteers is insufficient for operational reliability:

  • The solver pairs certified standbys to cover every primary slot.
  • Simulates dropping each assigned person individually.
  • If any single dropout causes an uncovered gap, status drops to NOT READY.
  • Guarantees the event can safely proceed regardless of morning cancellations.
Clear Terminology

Metric Definitions in Plain Words

Metric Name Mathematical Intent Plain Language Explanation
Role Coverage filled_must / total_must Percentage of critical volunteer slots for which a fully qualified volunteer has been matched and assigned.
Dropout Robustness survived_drops / total_slots The fraction of single-volunteer cancellations that the roster can absorb on event morning without leaving any role unstaffed.
Drive Ready Rate ready_drives / total_drives The proportion of scheduled community drives that satisfy both 100% role coverage and certified standby redundancy.
Repeat-Pick Share repeat_volunteers / total_assigned The share of roster positions filled by individuals who have already served in recent drives; lower values reflect fairer community inclusion.
Exposure Gini Coefficient G = ∑ |x_i - x_j| / (2n^2 μ) The exposure Gini coefficient measures inequality in volunteering assignments: a score closer to 0 indicates opportunities are shared evenly across registered youth, while higher scores show an unfair concentration on the same repeat participants.

Honest Engineering Boundaries

The SewaCircle engineering architecture is designed with strict epistemological modesty. We distinguish between mathematical guarantees and empirical realities:

1. Sample Boundary:

Tested on 10 synthetic drives and 80 volunteers across Uttar Pradesh. It is a research prototype, not an active statewide deployment.

2. Checked Skills ≠ Certificates:

Host checks mean a past field organizer affirmed attendance. They do not constitute formal government licenses or accredited degrees.

3. Ledger Integrity ≠ Ground Truth:

The SHA-256 hash chain proves that records were not secretly altered in storage. Truth originates solely from human host confirmation.
