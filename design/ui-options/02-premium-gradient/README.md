# 02-premium-gradient • SewaCircle Ready Crew

## Direction Concept (3 Sentences)
02-premium-gradient is a light, confident, and high-trust administrative console engineered for district youth organisers in Uttar Pradesh managing large-scale volunteering operations under tight time constraints. A soft, atmospheric gradient mesh across the landing hero and generous white cards with subtle elevation shadows establish an authoritative, institutional-grade product experience that treats civic service coordination as critical infrastructure. Through thin-weight display headlines, strict tabular figures, and prominent binary verdicts (READY / NOT READY), the interface transforms complex constraint satisfaction and single-dropout invariance proofs into serene, actionable operational decisions.

- **Primary Sans:** `Inter`, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif (weights 300, 400, 500, 600, 700 with `-0.03em` negative tracking on thin display titles)
- **Devanagari:** `Noto Sans Devanagari` (natural Hindi runs across hero titles, drive briefs, evidence spans, and volunteer greetings with `line-height >= 1.75` for unclipped conjunct rendering)
- **Tabular Monospace:** `JetBrains Mono`, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, Monaco, Consolas, monospace (strict `tnum` tabular numerals for candidate scores, travel distances, coordinates, task IDs, and SHA-256 block hashes)

## Key Interaction Ideas
- **High-Fidelity Pill Chrome:** Decisive, transactional pill buttons (`border-radius: 9999px`) with tight padding and subtle glow shadows that guide organizers through the four-stage lifecycle.
- **Heroic Binary Verdict Display:** Full-width hero banners for both `READY` and `NOT READY` states that clearly surface overall operational safety in a single glance before detailing individual roster rows.
- **Standby Redundancy Rail:** Visible side-by-side pairing of certified replacement volunteers to primary slots, proving mathematical resilience against morning dropouts.
- **One-Click Shareable WhatsApp Recruits:** Plain-text formatted copy blocks with instant clipboard actions, enabling organizers to broadcast exact volunteer recruitment criteria to local village messaging groups.
- **Human-in-the-Loop Discrepancy Auditing:** Visual distinction between benign informational flags (`round_number_inflation`) and operational discrepancies (`hours_exceed_recorded`), adhering to the core principle: *rules flag, the host decides*.
- **Cryptographic Merkle Linkage:** Nine linked SHA-256 blocks showing forward hash chaining from genesis (`0000...`) to current state, proving edit integrity without claiming artificial ground truth.
- **Original Vector UP District Schematic:** Geographically plotted vector schematic representing all 20 Uttar Pradesh administrative districts from approximate WGS84 coordinates, distinguishing 4 active drive hubs from 16 quiet sample nodes.
