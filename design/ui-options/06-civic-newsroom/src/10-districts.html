Why no borders This is a locator plot, not cartography. It places points by latitude and longitude and draws nothing between them. A district outline would imply a territorial claim the tool has no business making.
The sixteen

Agra, Meerut, Allahabad, Azamgarh, Ballia, Basti, Bareilly, Gonda, Jhansi, Mirzapur, Saharanpur, Sonbhadra, Sultanpur, Unnao, Gautam Buddha Nagar — and Kanpur Nagar.
