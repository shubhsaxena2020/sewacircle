01

The gap after Interested

Report · page one

MY Bharat collects intent at national scale. The step after a young person taps Interested is local, small and unforgiving: a team of six, one Saturday morning, a 25 km travel limit, a first-aider who is actually trained, and someone who can speak the village's dialect.

This prototype covers that step. It is a district tool, not a national platform: it takes the people who have already opted in, proposes the roles a drive needs, and builds a roster in which every essential role has a named standby. When it cannot, it says so, and prints the exact recruit request. After the drive it turns host notes into draft contribution cards, flags what does not add up, and records each step in a log.

All data in this document is synthetic: 80 volunteers, 10 drives, seed 7. It is not a claim about any real district, and it is not affiliated with MY Bharat or any government body.

05

What this does not claim

Limits, in plain words
  1. A hash chain shows edits, not truth. The log proves that a record was not quietly rewritten. Truth comes from the host's confirmation, not from the chain.
  2. The sample is synthetic. 80 volunteers, 10 drives, seed 7. No real person, family or minor appears anywhere in this document.
  3. It does not always succeed. Seven misses are printed on the evaluation page, including a drive where CP-SAT filled only 33% of must slots because nobody eligible was left.
  1. Safety roles are gated. First aid, disaster response and driving only accept skills the host has checked. A self-declared skill cannot fill them.
  2. Nothing is sent automatically. AI drafts. Deterministic rules decide. A human confirms, corrects or sends back. No message leaves the tool on its own.
  3. It complements, it does not replace. SewaCircle covers the step after a youth clicks Interested. It has no connection to MY Bharat, and it carries no government emblem or endorsement.
