🔍 Honest Reading & The 7 Evaluated Misses

What the Data Honestly Says

This test cohort consists of 10 synthetic tasks and 80 volunteers. The wide 95% confidence intervals reflect this sample size; this is an engineering benchmark, not a field trial.

The results clearly demonstrate that while nearest-skill greedy selection fills slots just as well (91.67% coverage), it completely neglects standby protection (only 15% robustness) and concentrates drives onto the same 40% familiar volunteers. CP-SAT achieves parity in coverage while drastically reducing volunteer burnout.
