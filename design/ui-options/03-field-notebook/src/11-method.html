Mathematical Method & Honest System Boundaries

How volunteer crews are decided: constraint satisfaction via OR-Tools CP-SAT, formal metric definitions, benchmark scale, and honest operational limitations.

OR-Tools CP-SAT Solver

⚙️ How a Crew is Decided: The Three-Tier Formulation

STAGE 1

Hard Feasibility Filter

Before solving, candidate volunteers must satisfy non-negotiable hard constraints:

  • Must hold required skill at/above minimum tier.
  • Free during the exact drive time slot.
  • Distance from site ≤ max travel radius (25.0 km).
  • Minors (under 18) allowed only if drive permits AND signed guardian consent is on file.
  • Safety roles (first aid, driving) accept host-checked skills only.
STAGE 2

Constraint Optimization

Google OR-Tools CP-SAT searches the assignment space across multi-objective hierarchical tiers:

  • Tier 1: Maximize filled must-have role slots.
  • Tier 2: Assign dedicated standby volunteers for every essential role.
  • Tier 3: Pair novices with experienced mentors.
  • Tier 4: Minimize repeat picks in the last 90 days and minimize travel distance.
STAGE 3

Dropout Survivability Check

The solver simulates the sudden absence of each primary volunteer:

  • Can the drive run if volunteer A no-shows?
  • Does an eligible standby exist without leaving another role uncovered?
  • Status: OPTIMAL (15 ms on task-01) certifies 100% single-dropout resilience.

📖 Metric Definitions in Plain Words

1. Role Coverage

The percentage of required essential roles staffed with qualified volunteers. If a drive requires 4 distinct roles and 4 are filled, coverage is 100%. If only 3 are filled, coverage is 75%.

2. Dropout Robustness

The probability that if any single crew member drops out on the morning of the drive, a pre-qualified standby is already in place to step in immediately without creating a secondary vacancy.

3. Ready Rate

The proportion of district drives that achieve a definitive READY verdict (100% must-role coverage AND 100% standby protection). If either condition fails, the verdict is NOT READY.

4. Repeat-Pick Share

The fraction of assigned roster slots given to volunteers who have already been mobilized in the preceding 90 days. Lower values indicate broader opportunity distribution across new youth.

⚖️ Honest Limitations & Boundaries

Sample & Regional Scope

Currently benchmarked on 10 synthetic tasks and 80 volunteers within one Indian state (Uttar Pradesh). Wider field conditions with multi-day transit remain out of scope.

Language & Extraction Recall

AI extraction models demonstrate a role recall of 0.932 (not 1.000). Ambiguous dialect mentions require human confirmation, as shown in the decision checklist.

Endorsement vs Certification

Skills are host-checked endorsements from previous community drives, not state medical licenses or academic diplomas.

Ledger Integrity vs Ground Truth

The cryptographic hash chain guarantees tamper-evident provenance of logged actions. Truth and physical reality originate solely from human host verification.
