📋 How The Field Notebook Operates (6 Sequential Steps)

STEP 01

1. Paste Drive Brief

Organiser inputs the field request in natural Hindi, English, or mixed Hinglish with date, block, and estimated scale.

STEP 02

2. AI Proposes, Host Confirms

Language model extracts required roles, evidence phrases, and confidence ratings. Human organiser reviews and adjusts.

STEP 03

3. Crew + Standby Redundancy

CP-SAT constraint solver pairs qualified volunteers and assigns dedicated standby backups for every single essential role.

STEP 04

4. Decisive Operational Verdict

Outputs READY if 100% covered and dropout-resilient, or NOT READY with exact recruit requests for WhatsApp mobilization.

STEP 05

5. Post-Drive Evidence & Flags

Drafts contribution cards from host notes. Fixed heuristic rules flag discrepancies against attendance records.

STEP 06

6. Cryptographic Ledger

Every draft, flag, and confirmation appends to a tamper-evident SHA-256 hash chain for honest, verifiable credentials.

⚖️ Honest Limitations of this System

1. Small Cohort & Synthetic Data

Tested on 10 synthetic drives and 80 youth volunteers in UP (seed 7). Wide confidence intervals reflect sample size; real-world field dynamics will present additional logistics challenges.

2. Language Pair Constraints

Language models process standard Hindi, English, and Hinglish. Dialects like Awadhi and Bhojpuri are detected and flagged for human organiser confirmation, never auto-assigned.

3. Host-Checked Endorsements

Safety roles (first aid, vehicle transport) accept host-checked skills from previous drives. These represent peer operational trust, not government-issued medical certifications.

4. Ledger Proves Audit, Not Truth

The SHA-256 hash chain mathematically guarantees that post-drive hours and contribution records were not altered after confirmation. Truth originates from the host, not the hash.
