What is Coverage?

Role Coverage

The proportion of mandatory roles that are staffed with qualified volunteers within the 25 km district travel radius.

What is Robustness?

Dropout Robustness

The percentage of roles that have an eligible standby volunteer ready to step in if someone drops out on short notice.

What is Fatigue?

Repeat Pick Share

The share of assignments assigned to volunteers picked within the last 90 days. Lower prevents relying repeatedly on the same youth.

What is Fairness?

Exposure Gini

The Gini coefficient of volunteer selections. 0.0 represents equal opportunity; lower ensures new volunteers get picked.
