Deterministic Operations Research

How the Crew is Decided.

SewaCircle Ready Crew replaces black-box heuristics with Google OR-Tools CP-SAT constraint programming organized into three strict sequential priority tiers.

Optimization Hierarchy — Sequential Constraint Programming Tiers
OR-Tools CP-SAT 9.15
Tier 1 (Hard Priority)

Safety & Feasibility

• Role qualifications: Volunteer skill level ≥ minimum required.
• Safety gate: First aid, driving, and disaster relief accept only host-checked skills.
• Distance constraint: Distance ≤ 25.0 km from task coordinates.
• Time slot: Volunteer marked available for drive time slot.
• Minor protection: Minors permitted only when guardian consent exists and task allows.

Tier 2 (Redundancy)

Dropout Robustness

• Standby guarantee: Pre-allocates eligible standby volunteers for every essential station.
• Single fault tolerance: If any one volunteer drops out, standby auto-promotes without role deficit.
• Overlapping standbys: Volunteers with dual skills (e.g. Awadhi + Posters) cover multiple roles.

Tier 3 (Equity & Optimization)

Fairness & Travel

• Fatigue penalty: Discourages picking youth chosen ≥ 3 times in past 90 days.
• Gini minimization: Distributes opportunities evenly across blocks.
• Mentor pairing: Pairs novices (0 past drives) with experienced volunteers (≥ 3 drives).
• Travel minimization: Prefers closer youth when skills are tied.

Solver Scalability: 80/80 optimal up to 500 volunteers in max 0.327 seconds on a standard single-core CPU. Zero cloud dependencies.

Operational Clarity

Metrics in Plain Words

District organisers should never have to decipher abstract academic formulas. Here is what every metric means on the ground:

Role Coverage

Current: 91.7%

What it asks: "Did we find someone qualified for every mandatory station?"
A score of 100% means the leader, first-aider, language speaker, and logistics helper are all present. If coverage is below 100%, the system flags NOT READY and names the exact missing skills.

Dropout Robustness

Current: 76.0%

What it asks: "If a volunteer's bicycle breaks down on Saturday morning, does the drive collapse?"
Robustness measures what percentage of roles have a standby volunteer ready to step in immediately. 100% robustness means any single person can drop out and the drive still runs.

Repeat Pick Share

Current: 23.0%

What it asks: "Are we burning out the same 5 hyper-active youth in the block?"
Measures the proportion of slots assigned to volunteers who were already picked recently. Lower numbers mean the tool actively opens doors for new volunteers instead of recycling favorites.

Exposure Gini

Current: 0.4677

What it asks: "Are volunteering opportunities distributed fairly across the district pool?"
The Gini coefficient measures opportunity inequality (0.0 = perfect equality). CP-SAT achieves 0.4677, significantly fairer than nearest-skill (0.5045) or manual picking (0.5081).

Boundaries & Ethics

Honest Institutional Limits

AI decision systems can overreach when boundaries are ambiguous. We state our limits explicitly:

No Psychological Profiling

SewaCircle does not score youth personality, dependability, or reliability using AI classifiers. Eligibility is strictly based on declared availability, distance, and verified certifications.

No Automated Commitment

No service hours or credentials are ever committed to the tamper-evident ledger automatically. A physical community host must confirm attendance logs before cryptographic seals are generated.

Non-Governmental Independence

SewaCircle Ready Crew is an independent research prototype designed to complement public volunteer platforms like MY Bharat. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government ministry.
