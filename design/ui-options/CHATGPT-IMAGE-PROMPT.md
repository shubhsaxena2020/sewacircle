WHAT THE PRODUCT IS (the aim)
SewaCircle Ready Crew is a planning tool for a district youth organiser in Uttar Pradesh, India. Young people have already said
they are interested in a volunteering drive (for example on India's national youth platform, MY Bharat, where youth click
"Interested"). The organiser still has to turn that list of willing people into a complete, safe crew. SewaCircle answers one
question: "Willing volunteers are here. Can this drive run?" It gives a clear verdict, READY or NOT READY, and when it is not
ready it says exactly who must still be recruited. It is a complement to MY Bharat, not a replacement, and not affiliated with it.
The user is a busy organiser, not an engineer: plain language, the verdict and the crew must be the heroes of the screen.

KEY FEATURES (show all of them across the pages)
- Paste a drive brief in Hindi, English or Hinglish; AI proposes the roles needed (with the phrase from the brief it came from and
  a confidence level); the organiser reviews and edits them. Personal details are masked before the AI sees the text.
- A solver builds the crew from interested youth: right skills, available on the day, within travel distance, guardian consent
  for 16 to 17 year olds, fair rotation (not the same people every time), and newcomers paired with experienced volunteers.
- A standby for every essential role, so any one person can drop out without the drive failing.
- Safety roles (first aid, disaster response, driving) only accept skills a host has checked, never self-declared ones.
- The verdict: READY, or NOT READY plus exact recruit requests with a "copy / share" action.
- "Why not this person?" explains why someone was not picked. "Simulate a drop-out" shows how the crew heals.
- Four planning methods compared side by side: SewaCircle, nearest-skill matching, a careful human organiser, random.
- After the drive: AI drafts contribution cards from the host's notes, fixed rules flag conflicts with attendance, a host confirms,
  corrects or sends a card back for revision. Nothing is auto-approved.
- A tamper-evident log (a hash chain) records every decision, with a "verify" check and a credential export.
- Hindi and English language toggle. A projector mode with large type for presenting.
- Every page shows a small "Synthetic data" badge and the line "Not affiliated with MY Bharat or any government body."
