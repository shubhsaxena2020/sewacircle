SEWACIRCLE UI OPTIONS - THE 8 DIRECTIONS (each folder gets exactly one)

01-precision-dark  | inspiration file: awesome-design-md/design-md/linear.app/DESIGN.md
   Dark, exact, quiet. Near-black surfaces, one cool accent, hairline borders, monospace for ids and hashes, a command bar
   (Ctrl+K) in the console header, keyboard hints. The verdict glows softly; everything else is restrained.
02-premium-gradient | inspiration: stripe/DESIGN.md
   Light, premium, confident. A soft animated-looking gradient mesh in the landing hero (static CSS), deep navy type, generous
   white cards with subtle shadows, crisp data visualisation, editorial section headers.
03-field-notebook | inspiration: notion/DESIGN.md
   Warm off-white document feel. The console reads like a living planning document: blocks, toggles, callouts, inline tags,
   checklists. Serif headings with a clean sans body. Calm, literate, unhurried.
04-community-warm | inspiration: airbnb/DESIGN.md
   Friendly and people-first. Rounded cards, initial-avatars for volunteers, a warm coral accent, large friendly headings, the
   crew shown as faces in role slots, soft illustration in SVG. Feels like a community app, not admin software.
05-minimal-keynote | inspiration: apple/DESIGN.md
   One idea per screen. Huge type, vast whitespace, near-monochrome with a single accent, cinematic section rhythm on the landing
   page, product-shot style framing of console panels, extremely restrained UI chrome.
06-civic-newsroom | inspiration: theverge/DESIGN.md and wired/DESIGN.md
   Bold editorial magazine. Giant condensed headlines, strong grid, high contrast, numbered sections, pull quotes from the data,
   the verdict set like a front-page headline, data tables styled like an investigative feature.
07-ops-whiteboard | inspiration: miro/DESIGN.md
   A planning board. The console is a canvas: roles are columns or frames, volunteers are cards placed into slots, standbys sit
   behind, gaps are dashed empty slots, recruit requests are sticky notes. Toolbar, zoom chip, minimap. Visual, spatial, tactile.
08-mitti-original | no brand file: ORIGINAL direction
   Indian craft, made modern. Handmade-paper texture (CSS noise), indigo, terracotta and turmeric palette (NO tricolour, no flag
   colours as branding), block-print style borders and dividers drawn in SVG, Devanagari-forward headings with English
   secondary, warm and dignified, not kitsch. Must still read as a precise, trustworthy tool.
