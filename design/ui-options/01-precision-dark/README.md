# 01-precision-dark • SewaCircle Ready Crew

## Direction Concept
A dark, quiet, software-craft console engineered for district youth organizers in Uttar Pradesh managing high-stakes volunteer deployments under time pressure. Dense volunteer rosters, constraint satisfaction metrics, and regional dialect requirements are framed within near-black charcoal surfaces, hairline borders, and monospace data typography so operational choices remain calm, exact, and authoritative. The binary READY and NOT READY verdicts glow softly as the visual heroes of the screen, backed by verified standby redundancy, interactive why-not reasoning, and tamper-evident cryptographic audit logs.

## Palette
- **Canvas:** `#08090a` (deepest near-black workspace background)
- **Surfaces:** `#0f1011` (surface-1), `#141516` (surface-2), `#18191a` (surface-3), `#1d1e20` (surface-4)
- **Hairlines & Borders:** `#23252a` (hairline border), `#2d3038` (subtle border), `#3e424d` (strong border)
- **Ink Typography:** `#f7f8f8` (primary text), `#d0d6e0` (muted body text), `#9ea6b2` (subtle / metadata text)
- **Chromatic Accent:** `#5e6ad2` (cool lavender-indigo), `#7b86e8` (hover), `#9aa5ff` (accent text)
- **Status Semantics:**
  - **Ready / Verified:** `#34d399` (emerald glow, > 10:1 contrast)
  - **Not Ready / Caution:** `#fbbf24` (amber glow, > 11:1 contrast)
  - **Action Required / Gap:** `#f87171` / `#fca5a5` (high-contrast alert text on `#2b1114` dark burgundy background)

## Typography
- **Primary Sans:** `Inter`, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif (balanced neutral sans for high-density UI chrome and operational legibility)
- **Monospace:** `JetBrains Mono`, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, Monaco, Consolas, monospace (tabular numerals for candidate scores, travel distances, coordinates, task IDs, and SHA-256 block hashes)
- **Devanagari:** `Noto Sans Devanagari` (line-height ≥ 1.75 for fluid, natural Hindi rendering across hero copy, drive briefs, evidence spans, and volunteer greetings)

## Key Interaction Ideas
- **Command Bar (`Ctrl+K`):** Global header search and action palette enabling fast keyboard traversal across scheduled drives, volunteer rosters, and cryptographic ledger verification.
- **Glowing Binary Verdicts:** Soft radial emerald (`● READY`) and amber (`● NOT READY`) heroes that immediately communicate whether a drive is mathematically safe to run.
- **Standby Redundancy Rail:** Visible side-by-side mapping of verified standby volunteers to primary slots, proving single-point-of-failure invariance.
- **Why-Not & Drop-Out Simulator:** Real-time algorithmic inspection cards showing why an alternate candidate was ranked lower and proving how standbys automatically heal dropped slots.
- **Deterministic Discrepancy Resolution:** Visual distinction between informational quality flags (`round_number_inflation`) and operational discrepancies (`hours_exceed_recorded`), maintaining the human-in-the-loop principle where rules flag and hosts decide.
- **Tamper-Evident SHA-256 Ledger:** Cryptographically linked blocks displaying `prev_hash` to `entry_hash` transitions with one-click copy affordances and credential export.
