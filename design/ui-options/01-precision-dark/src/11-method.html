Solver Architecture & Operational Principles

How SewaCircle evaluates feasibility, redundancy, and equity without ungrounded algorithmic claims.

Engine: OR-Tools CP-SAT (15 ms)
Three-Tier Pipeline

How the Crew is Decided

A three-stage deterministic process: non-negotiable feasibility boundaries, mathematical slot optimization, and dropout invariance certification.

STAGE 01 Hard Filters

Feasibility Constraints

A candidate must satisfy all physical criteria before the solver evaluates suitability:

  • Skill Match: Must possess required skill at or above minimum level.
  • Time Slot: Must be explicitly free during drive slot (e.g., sat_morning).
  • Travel Radius: Candidate distance must not exceed max_travel_km (25 km).
  • Minor Safety: Youth under 18 admitted only if allow_minors is true and guardian consent is logged.
  • Safety Roles: First-aid, disaster response, and driving accept host-checked skills only; self-declared claims are rejected.
STAGE 02 CP-SAT Optimization

Slot & Standby Allocation

Constraint satisfaction solver (Google OR-Tools CP-SAT) assigns primary slots and standbys:

  • Tier 1 — Essential Roles: Fill every required "must" slot with qualified candidates.
  • Tier 2 — Standby Redundancy: Pair an unconflicted standby backup for every essential slot.
  • Tier 3 — Proximity & Rotation: Minimize travel distance and penalize recent repeat picks to reduce burnout.
  • Tier 4 — Novice Mentorship: Pair newcomers (0 past drives) with experienced volunteers.
STAGE 03 Invariance Proof

Single-Dropout Verification

Re-checks mathematical safety across all possible failure modes:

  • No-Show Simulation: Tests what happens if any primary volunteer drops out on Saturday morning.
  • Automatic Promotion: Validates that the assigned standby covers the gap without causing secondary deficits.
  • Binary Verdict: If any essential role is left unprotected, status drops to NOT READY with exact recruit needs.
  • Execution Speed: Solved in 15 ms on task-01 with status OPTIMAL.

Honest Engineering

System Boundaries & Limitations

Clear delineation of what the tool cannot do to prevent unwarranted reliance.

Cohort Sample Size

Evaluated on 10 synthetic tasks and 80 volunteers in Uttar Pradesh. Wide confidence intervals reflect sample constraints; not a substitute for field trial data.

Language Tuning

NLP extraction is trained on Hindi, English, and Hinglish. Regional dialect disambiguation requires human organiser sign-off.

Skill Verification Limits

"Host-checked" skills represent past organiser observations, not formal accreditation or legal certifications.

Ledger Integrity vs Truth

The SHA-256 ledger proves audit log integrity and edit attribution. It proves what was recorded, not what physically occurred; ground truth requires host confirmation.

NLP Recall Bounds

Role extraction achieves 0.932 recall, not 1.0. Critical roles may be omitted in ambiguous text, making organiser review essential.
