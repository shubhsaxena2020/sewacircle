METRIC DEFINITIONS IN PLAIN LANGUAGE
Role Coverage: The fraction of essential drive roles successfully matched with qualified volunteers.
Dropout Robustness: The share of primary roles that have an independent, qualified standby assigned so that a no-show leaves no gap.
Ready Rate: Percentage of scheduled drives that achieve 100% coverage AND complete single-dropout safety.
Repeat-Pick Share: Proportion of assignments given to youth who already volunteered recently; lower means broader community engagement.
Exposure Gini: A standard index (0 to 1) measuring inequality in how volunteer opportunities are distributed; lower score indicates fairer access for all youth.

HONEST READING & EMPIRICAL SCOPE

This benchmark was generated using n=10 tasks and 80 volunteers from the synthetic dataset (seed 7). The wide confidence intervals reflect the modest sample size. While the CP-SAT solver demonstrates superior dropout resilience and fairness compared to greedy and nearest-skill heuristics, it cannot manufacture candidates where demographic deficits exist (as seen in task-04 and task-05). This benchmark serves as an algorithmic verification, not a multi-district operational field trial.
