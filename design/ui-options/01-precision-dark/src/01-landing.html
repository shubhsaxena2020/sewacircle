Operational Flow

How SewaCircle Builds a Ready Crew

Six disciplined steps from unstructured WhatsApp drive briefs to immutable cryptographic logs.

01 • BRIEF Natural Text

Paste Drive Brief

The district organiser pastes the drive announcement in natural Hindi, English, or Hinglish with location, time, and crowd estimates.

02 • ROLES AI Extraction

Propose & Confirm

AI proposes required roles with exact evidence spans and confidence scores. The organiser reviews dialect, counts, and safety constraints.

03 • SOLVER CP-SAT Optimization

Crew + Standby Pairing

The solver assigns qualified volunteers within the travel radius and pairs verified standby coverage for every safety-critical role.

04 • VERDICT Binary Gate

READY or NOT READY

An unequivocal verdict. If NOT READY, the tool generates precise recruit requests with exact skills and travel radiuses to share on WhatsApp.

05 • EVIDENCE Deterministic Rules

Cards & Conflict Flags

After the drive, AI drafts contribution cards from host notes. Fixed rules flag discrepancies against attendance records; the host confirms.

06 • LEDGER SHA-256 Chain

Tamper-Evident History

Every action is recorded in a cryptographically linked ledger. Proves audit integrity and editing history without trusting central claims.

Design Transparency

Operational Limits & Boundaries

What SewaCircle can and cannot do. We state system boundaries clearly to avoid ungrounded assumptions.

Sample Size & Synthetic Scope

Current evaluation is run on 10 synthetic tasks and 80 volunteers across Uttar Pradesh (seed 7). Wide confidence intervals reflect this cohort size.

Language Coverage

Role extraction is currently optimized for Hindi, English, and Hinglish drive briefs. Regional dialects like Awadhi and Bhojpuri are flagged for organiser disambiguation.

Host-Checked Endorsements

Safety-critical roles (first aid, transport) require host-checked claims from prior drives, not official government degrees or medical licenses.

Audit Trail vs. Ground Truth

The SHA-256 cryptographic ledger proves tamper-evident edit history and attribution. It does not certify physical reality; ground truth originates from human host confirmation.
