# SewaCircle - 9 continuation clips for Gemini (Veo). One film, told in paper.

HOW TO GENERATE
- 16:9, the longest length Gemini allows (8 to 15 s). Generate in order, 1 to 9.
- For clips 2 to 9: if Gemini lets you add an image, upload the LAST FRAME of the previous clip as the starting image (pause at
  the end, screenshot or save the frame), then paste the prompt. This keeps the film continuous.
- Paste the STYLE LOCK paragraph first, then the clip text, every time.
- Save as c1.mp4 ... c9.mp4 in this folder. Regenerate any clip with readable words, real faces, logos, flags or emblems.

STYLE LOCK (paste before every clip)
Handcrafted paper-cut stop-motion diorama, layered matte paper with visible fibres and soft cast shadows between layers, warm
morning-to-evening natural light, palette of indigo, terracotta, turmeric yellow, leaf green and off-white paper, subtle
miniature tilt-shift depth of field, gentle stop-motion movement at 12 to 15 frames per second feel, calm slow camera. People
are simple faceless paper figures. No readable text or letters anywhere, no logos, no flags, no national emblems, no religious
symbols, no red cross symbol. Cohesive, dignified, warm.

c1 - Dawn in the village
A paper-cut village in rural Uttar Pradesh wakes at dawn: layered mustard fields, a slow paper river, small houses with
terracotta roofs, a hand water pump and a wooden notice board in the village square. The camera drifts slowly forward over the
fields and settles on the empty notice board in the square. Ends: notice board centred, soft sunrise behind it.

c2 - A drive is announced
Continue from the notice board. A folded paper notice unfolds itself and pins onto the board (only abstract lines, no words).
From the lanes, about twelve faceless paper figures of young people walk in and gather in front of the board; a small warm
glowing dot appears above each one as they arrive, showing they are interested. Ends: a loose crowd of twelve figures with
glowing dots, camera slightly higher.

c3 - Interest becomes a plan
Continue from the crowd. The twelve glowing dots lift off and rise into the sky above the village and arrange themselves into a
large floating paper ring with six round empty sockets, like a constellation made of paper. The figures below look up. Ends: the
six-socket ring hanging in the sky, sockets empty and softly lit.

c4 - The crew takes shape
Continue from the ring. One by one, four paper figures rise up and settle into four sockets with a soft warm pulse each; each
carries a tiny symbol cut from paper: a poster, a small heart with a bandage, a box of supplies, a speech bubble. One socket
stays empty and pulses amber, and the sixth stays dim. Ends: ring with four filled sockets, one amber pulsing gap.

c5 - The bench and the gap
Continue. Behind three of the filled sockets a smaller paper pin slides in and docks with a click, a backup for that role. The
socket with the heart symbol has no backup pin and glows softly amber. Far below, a new paper figure walks in from the edge of
the fields, rises and fills the amber pulsing gap. Ends: ring complete, backups docked, one socket still faintly amber.

c6 - Ready
Continue. The whole ring warms to turmeric gold. From above, a large round paper seal with a raised check mark slowly presses
down onto the centre of the ring, releasing a gentle burst of paper marigold petals. Ends: the sealed gold ring glowing calmly in
the sky.

c7 - The drive happens
Continue. The sealed ring descends gently to the village square and becomes a circle on the ground; the paper figures take their
places: one sets up a handwashing station at the water pump, one pins up plain posters, one hands out supplies, one talks with
elders. A time-lapse: the paper sun arcs across the sky and the shadows swing. Ends: warm dusk light over the busy square.

c8 - What each person did
Continue at dusk. From each volunteer figure a small paper card floats up and drifts toward a host figure holding a paper
lantern; the cards stack, then link one by one into a chain of glowing paper blocks joined by thin threads. Ends: a softly
glowing chain of paper blocks over the square at dusk.

c9 - Many villages, one question
Continue. The camera pulls far back: the village becomes one small point on a large hand-drawn paper map of Uttar Pradesh made of
many districts; across the map, small rings light up, most turning gold, a few pulsing amber. Keep the upper third of the frame
as calm empty night-blue paper sky for a title. Ends: wide map glowing, holding still for three seconds.
