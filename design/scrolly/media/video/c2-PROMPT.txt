CLIP 2 - A drive is announced:
Start exactly on this frame: the empty paper notice board centred in the village square, sunrise behind it, the hand water pump
on the right. A folded paper notice slides up from below the frame, unfolds itself and pins onto the board with two small paper
pins (only abstract lines on it, no words). Then, from the lanes on both sides, faceless paper figures of young people (about ten
to twelve, in kurtas and simple shirts in terracotta, indigo and turmeric paper) walk in calmly and gather in a loose semicircle
in front of the board. As each one arrives, a small warm glowing paper dot lights up above their head, showing they are
interested. The camera slowly rises a little and pulls back so the whole group and the board are visible.
End frame: the notice board with the pinned notice, a loose semicircle of about twelve paper figures each with a glowing dot
above them, sunrise light, camera slightly higher than the start.
